Objective

Label 30 e-commerce product reviews with sentiment and issue category to support customer-experience reporting, then run a self-QA pass to catch and correct labeling errors before delivery.

The dataset represents a typical raw export from a review platform: varied review length, mixed tone, and reviews that combine more than one topic — which is exactly where labeling guidelines matter most.

Labels Applied

Sentiment: Positive / Negative / Neutral  ·  Category: Product Quality / Shipping / Customer Service / Price / Packaging  ·  Confidence: High / Medium / Low (self-flagged for review)

Process

  1. Read the labeling guidelines and built a written rubric before touching the data.
  2. First-pass labeling of all 30 records against that rubric.
  3. Self-QA pass: re-checked every "Low" and "Medium" confidence row, plus a random 20% sample of "High" confidence rows.
  4. Logged results, corrections, and final accuracy in a separate QA summary.
